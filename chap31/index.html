📲 To install: tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen”.

How to play: you are a sausage dog on a peaceful walk, seen over your own shoulder as the owner, holding the lead. Walk over every bone in the garden to finish. W / ↑ walk, S / ↓ back, ← → (or A / D) turn, Q / E side-step, hold Shift to trot (faster, but animals notice you from further away). You can also drag on the picture to turn. Squirrels dart about, cats sit and stare, and dog walkers stroll the paths. If one is too close and can see you the Nervous bar fills, then you bark and lose peace. Stay behind hedges, wait for them to pass, take another path. Steaks restore peace. Pick a costume (it shows on your portrait and on screen), a coat, a garden size and a difficulty. Daily gives everyone the same garden today. P pauses, M toggles the little map.
