📲 To install: tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen”.

How to play: find every word in the list. Words can run in up to 8 directions (across, down, backwards, diagonal). Drag from the first letter to the last, or tap the first letter and then the last. Found words get coloured. In settings you can change the grid size (8 to 25), how many words (4 to 20), the longest word, and whether letters are CAPITALS. Daily gives everyone the same puzzle today. Copy as text puts the grid and word list on your clipboard so you can paste it into a document and print it.
