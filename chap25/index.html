How to play: your pig is flying, but pigs are not good at it. Every tap makes the wing flap and lifts the pig a little, then gravity pulls it down again. Fly through the gaps between the pillars without touching them, the ground or the top. Each gap passed is 1 point. Grab 🍎 apples for fun and the golden ✨ halo for one free bump. Easy has wide gaps, Hard is fast and narrow, and Daily gives everyone the same course for the day. Keys: Space, ↑ or W to flap, P to pause, R to restart.
