📲 To install: tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen”.

How to play: race 3 laps as fast as you can. ← → (or A / D) steer, ↑ (or W) is gas, ↓ (or S) is brake, Space fires the jets and makes the car jump; air blasts out under all four wheels. Fly over other cars to pass them safely and score a bonus. Pick a Scene (meadow, city, sunset coast, hills and cliffs), a Map (road shape) and a Level (traffic). Land on one, or hit a tree or sign, and you crash and slow down. Driving on the grass is slow. P pauses, R restarts. On a phone use the buttons under the picture (turn on Auto gas in settings if you like).
