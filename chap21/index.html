📜 Chronicle: the wars and the generals · 戰史與名將 (EN / 繁 / 简 / 日 / 한)
📲 To install: tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen”.

How to play: enemies march along the road from the top to your gate at the bottom. Tap a green hex tile and pick a general: each carries his own weapon from the novel 三國演義. Tap a general on the map to upgrade or sell him. Towers on a ▲ hill see further and hit harder. Cao Cao boosts the 6 tiles around him. Generals of your own kingdom (★) hit 10% harder. Flying enemies (ghosts, drones) can only be hit by towers marked ✈. Tap a commander’s portrait at the top for a big power. Keys: Space = start wave, P = pause, 1 2 3 = hero powers, U = upgrade, S = sell.
