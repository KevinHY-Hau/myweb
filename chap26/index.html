How to play: this is the old Chinese puzzle 華容道 (Klotski). Only the big red block (曹) has to leave, through the red gap in the wall. Every other piece stays on the board: slide them around to make room. Drag a piece sideways or up and down, any distance in a straight line counts as one move. Press Hint if you are stuck. Every puzzle is made by a program that works backwards from the finished board, so it always has an answer.
