📲 To install: tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen”.

How to play: pick 5, 6, 7 or 8 letters and guess the hidden word. Longer words get one extra try per letter (5 letters = 6 tries, 8 letters = 9). After each guess every tile turns green (right letter, right place), yellow (letter is in the word, wrong place) or grey (not in the word). Type on your keyboard or tap the keys; Enter sends, Backspace deletes. Hint reveals one letter (once per word). Daily gives everyone the same word today. In settings you can switch to colour-blind colours or only allow words from the list.
