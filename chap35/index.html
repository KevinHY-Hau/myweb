📲 To install: tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen”.

How to play: drag on the table to aim (the white line shows where the cue ball goes and the yellow line where the ball it hits will go). Set the power, then press SHOOT (or Space). The arrows (or ← →) nudge your aim a tiny bit; ↑ ↓ change power. Spin: drag the dot on the little ball. Up = follow (the cue ball runs on after hitting), down = draw (it pulls back), left or right = side spin that changes how it bounces off a cushion. Double-tap the little ball to centre the dot. 8-ball: pot all your balls (solids 1–7 or stripes 9–15, set by your first pot), then the black 8. Keep your turn by potting one of yours. Foul (cue ball potted, no ball hit, or the wrong ball hit first) = the other side places the cue ball anywhere. Potting the 8 too early loses. Trick shots: each puzzle has a goal; the balls reset after every miss. Fewer tries = more stars. The Hint button shows one way (and costs the stars).
