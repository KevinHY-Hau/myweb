📲 To install: tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen”.

How to play: tap one of your pieces, then tap a green square to move. Capturing is compulsory: if you can jump, you must (the pieces that can are ringed in red). A jump can carry on: keep tapping the next square until the move is finished. Reach the far row to be crowned a king (♛). Classic rules: men move and capture forward, kings go one square either way. Flying kings: kings slide any distance and men may also capture backwards. You win when the other side cannot move. 40 moves each without a capture or a man moving is a draw. Pick the board size under the board (6, 8, 10 or 12), and the computer's level in the settings.
