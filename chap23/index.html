📲 To install: tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen”.

Rules: take turns dropping a disc into a column. It falls to the lowest free spot. The first player to line up 4 discs, across, up and down, or diagonally, wins. If the board fills up, it is a draw. Tip: the middle column is the strongest, and look out for two threats at once. Tap a column to drop. Keys: 1 to 9 drop in that column, Z undo, H hint, N new game.
